02 Printing

Sheets are 1680 × 1188 px, A3 landscape at ~4 px/mm. Print at 100% on A3 (297 × 420 mm). On US Letter, print at ~71% as a shop reference — do not scale W-10 templates on Letter unless you re-check the 1.00″ bar.

  • W-1 General arrangement — hang this first.
  • W-2 … W-7 Frame, drum, elevation, conveyor, drive, dust.
  • W-8 / W-14 BOM and buy list. Catalog numbers drift; the search term is the spec.
  • W-9 Exploded groups and datums.
  • W-10 1:1 disc, plate, key. Measure the scale bar on paper before you cut.
  • W-11 Optional 32″ cabinet.
  • W-12 Wiring ladder — design intent, not a permit drawing.
  • W-13 Abrasive wrap development.

Fab templates T-DISC.svg and T-PLATE.svg are millimetre 1:1. Print those on Letter/A4 at 100% with “actual size” — no fit-to-page.

03 CAD

You do not need FreeCAD or OpenSCAD to get a solid model. Python 3 writes STL and OBJ from the kernel. Open the assembly in any mesh viewer, slicer, or CAD that imports STL.

python3 scripts/gen_walter_cad.py
python3 scripts/gen_walter_plans.py
python3 scripts/gen_walter_fab.py
python3 scripts/gen_walter_manual.py
python3 scripts/gen_walter_drawings.py
python3 scripts/gen_walter_planforge.py
python3 scripts/verify_walter.py

Or one shot: ./scripts/build_walter.sh

If you change a dimension, change it in walter_kernel.py only, then regenerate. Do not edit parameters.scad or parameters.json by hand.

04 Buying and building

Order of spend: plywood → shaft/bearings → motor → conveyor belt → Acme → electrics → abrasive. A used SuperMax 16-32 is $1,800–2,400; this package is $1,100–1,600 in 2026 hobby-shop pricing if you already own a collector and a tablesaw.

Do not buy: MDF for drum or walls; a 16×48 sanding belt as a conveyor; PVC pipe rollers; pillow blocks on a single ¾″ wall with no plate; an open-drip motor in this dust; a light switch as the only disconnect; Formica for the platen.

Electrics: 115 V 20 A dedicated, magnetic starter with no-volt release, mushroom E-stop in series with the coil. Have an electrician size the overload heaters to motor FLA. W-12 is not a UL listing.
