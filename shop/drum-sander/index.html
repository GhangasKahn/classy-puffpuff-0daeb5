Why the cut was uneven

Three planes

Rev A could be parallel once. Paper wrap, a racking table, and a board that lifts into the drum undo that. Quality is not one shim — it is table plane, drum axis, and feed vector staying coincident while you cut.

01 Table plane

Torsion box + ways

Two ¾″ skins and a 4″ rib grid, then a known-flat wear face. UHMW ways take feed thrust so the Acme screws only lift. Diagonals ≤ 0.004″.

02 Drum axis

Pack-bore, then re-clock

Discs bored as a pack. Drive bearing locked, idler floating. True on a full-width sled to 0.002″ TIR. Wrap paper — then indicator A/B. Paper is not uniform.

03 Feed vector

Hold-downs + dual lift

Spring rollers keep stock on the table (ease outfeed for leading snipe). Chain-coupled screws rise together; uncouple left for taper, home dog to return.

  • 1/40 turn of ½-10 Acme ≈ 0.0025″ at that end — fine enough to chase 0.003″.
  • Pass schedule: 0.008″ @ 80 · 0.004″ @ 120 · 0.001″ @ 180/220. Caliper four corners.
  • Optional ⅛″ slow oscillator (~80 cpm, not drum RPM) erases the helical tracks of a 3″ spiral wrap.
  • Taper mode is a feature: uncouple left, drop B, sand, return to the home dog.

Why rebuild it

Lessons baked in

Walters’ walkthrough documents three failed conveyors, MDF cracks, and a wish for an adjustable idler. Rev A deleted the conveyor. Rev B treats parallelism as a kinematic system, not a shim.

  • Dual ½-10 Acme, chain-coupled — table stays coplanar while it rises.
  • Left clutch + home dog: taper when you want it, parallel when you don’t.
  • UHMW ways + torsion-box table + phenolic/MIC-6 wear face.
  • Floating idler bearing so the shaft is not a three-force beam.
  • Spring hold-downs (Jet/Grizzly practice) — snipe is a roller problem, not a drum problem.
  • True → wrap → re-clock. Optional slow oscillation for finish work.
01 Frame

Baltic birch box

Stack-drill sides, UHMW ways, dual Acme in bronze nuts. Mid stretchers stay; ways locate the table.

02 Drum

True in place

Pack-bore, float the idler, true on a ways-riding sled to 0.002″ TIR — then wrap paper and clock A/B.

03 Feed

Manual, controlled

Hold-downs keep the board on the table. You still hear the motor. Chip load stays even without a conveyor.
