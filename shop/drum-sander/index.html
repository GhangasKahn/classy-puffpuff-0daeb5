Engineering

Rev A specification

Dedicated motor, Baltic birch frame, flange bearings, solid sliding table. Sized for panels up to 15.5″ — fence boards, cutting-board blanks, veneer leveling.

Capacity15.5″ wide · 1/16″–3″ thick · ~12″ min length (manual feed)

Why rebuild it

Lessons baked in

Walters’ YouTube walkthrough and woodgears write-up document three failed conveyor attempts, MDF drum cracks, and a wish for an adjustable idler bearing. Rev A treats those as requirements.

  • Solid laminated sliding table — conveyor deleted. Push sticks and light passes.
  • Idler-side flange bearing on jack screws / shims for parallelism or intentional taper.
  • ½ HP motor (was ⅓) with gravity belt tension and a lock so the belt cannot pump the cradle.
  • Precision shaft + 1 mm disc relief every four MDF discs (or all-birch drum option).
  • Replaceable wear surface; elongated washers under star knobs; oak bearing locators.
01 Frame

Matched side panels, three stretchers, flange bearings. Mid stretcher carries the elevating nut.

02 Drum

True in place

Stack, glue, key, then sand the drum round against the table plane — the machine trues itself.

03 Feed

Manual, controlled

You hear the motor. Back off when it bogs. Consistent enough for thicknessing without conveyor drama.
