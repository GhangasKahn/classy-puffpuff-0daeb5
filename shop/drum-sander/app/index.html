Machine in one paragraph

A 36×22×28″ Baltic-birch cabinet holds a 1.5 HP 56-frame motor. Two 3/4″ towers carry 1″ pillow blocks. A 6″ × 18″ drum on a 24″ shaft is driven ~1:2 by an A-section V-belt (~860 RPM). The 20×22″ table rides four 1/2-13 jacks through 2.5″ of travel. A sheet-metal hood dumps into a 4″ collector port. Wrap 18″ PSA paper; max take is 1/32″ hardwood per pass.

Safety

  • Never reach under a spinning drum. Stop, wait, then adjust jacks.
  • Dust collection on before any cut. Fine sanding dust is combustible.
  • Wrap PSA with the drum unplugged. Overlap ~1/4″, no wrinkles.
  • Magnetic switch so a power blip does not restart the drum.

Twelve shop steps

Assembly guide

Carcass, drum, table, finish. Check items off as you go — progress stays on this device.

01

Nest and cut plywood

Lay out DS-2 on two 4×8 × 3/4″ Baltic birch sheets. Cut sides, front, back, bottom, motor shelf, table, aprons, and tower cheeks.

02

Assemble the cabinet

Glue and screw the 36×22×28 carcass. Bottom sits in a 3/8″ dado. Square diagonals before the glue sets.

03

Motor shelf and wiring chase

Install the 14″-deep shelf 8″ off the bottom on the left. Bore a 1.5″ belt slot in the left wall.

04

Stand the bearing towers

Bolt 6×14 cheeks to the cabinet top, 19″ apart inside. They must be coplanar.

05

Align pillow blocks

Set both 1″ pillow blocks. Use a dummy rod. Shim until the shaft spins with no bind.

06

Build the drum on the shaft

Hubs in the 6″ tube, 24″ shaft through, lock collars outboard. Wrap PSA later.

07

Hang the drum

Drop the shaft into the pillow blocks. End play ≤ 0.010″. TIR within 0.015″.

08

Drive and belt

1.5 HP on the shelf, 2″ and 4″ pulleys, A-36 belt. ~1/2″ mid-span deflection.

09

Table and four jacks

20×22 table, T-nuts at corners, 1/2-13 jacks with handwheels. 2.5″ travel.

10

Fence and stops

3×20 fence 90° to the drum. UHMW wear strip on the infeed lip.

11

Dust hood and 4″ port

Wrap the hood with ~3/8″ drum clearance. Port exits the back. Collector on.

12

Wire, wrap, true, test

Magnetic switch + E-stop. 80-grit PSA. First pass: scrap pine, 1/64″ take.
