Clock A/B (paper on)

  1. Disconnect power — Unplug. Hood off. Paper off for TIR; paper on for A/B parallel.
  2. Seat the table in the ways — Raise/lower through full travel. No bind, no rock. Winding sticks on wear face — no twist.
  3. Drum TIR — Indicator on mid-span OD. Rotate by hand. If > 0.002″, re-true on the sled before wrapping paper.
  4. Wrap & re-clock — Velcro then spiral paper. Paper is not uniform — A/B will change. This is the measurement that matters.
  5. A/B parallel — Same indicator height, drive end then idler. Uncouple left Acme; 1/40 turn ≈ 0.0025″. Recouple. Set home dog.
  6. Hold-down height — Feelers under each roller vs drum. 0.030″ below. Leading snipe → ease outfeed spring; trailing snipe → ease infeed.
  7. Witness board — 6″ × 16″ maple, 80 grit, one pass. Ridge at overlap = idler high/low. Caliper corners. Log in the Build app.
  8. Taper mode (optional) — Uncouple left, drop idler a few thousandths, sand, then return to home dog — do not re-invent parallel each time.

Assembly

  1. A1 Template & stack-drill sides — Clamp both side panels face-to-face. Drill bearing CLs, way screw holes, and indicator pad as one stack so left and right are identical.
  2. A2 Assemble box + UHMW ways — Glue + screw stretchers. Square diagonals. Bond UHMW ways to inner faces; they are the table’s only vertical reference.
  3. A3 Pack-bore discs & laminate drum — Bandsaw oversize. Stack all discs in the bore jig; drill/ream ⌀¾″ as a pack. Key with piano wire. 1 mm relief every 4 MDF discs. Static-balance end discs.
  4. A4 Fixed drive bearing, floating idler — Lock the drive flange. Idler flange sits on an axial-float pad (slots or liner) so the shaft cannot banana from over-constraint or heat.
  5. A5 Motor cradle, coplanar pulleys, lock — Straightedge across pulley faces. Gravity tension, then lock the cradle so the belt cannot pump.
  6. A6 Torsion-box table + wear face — Skins + 4″ rib grid, continuous glue. Flatten the top, then bond phenolic or tooling plate. Check diagonals to 0.004″.
  7. A7 Dual Acme lift + chain couple — Bronze nuts in table. Chain both ½-10 screws. Fit left clutch and parallel-home dog. Table must rise without twist in the ways.
  8. A8 Hold-down roller yokes — Infeed and outfeed rollers on spring yokes. Set 0.030″ below drum OD (paper on). Too much spring = snipe.
  9. A9 Kerf-bend dust hood — Kerf ¼″ ply, wet, glue to form, fill kerfs. 4″ port. Hood must clear oscillator stroke if fitted.
  10. A10 True drum on full-width sled — Abrasive face-up on the truing sled that rides the ways. Light passes until TIR ≤ 0.002″. Then Velcro + spiral paper.
  11. A11 Indicator clock A/B — parallel home — Paper on. Indicator at drive (A) and idler (B). Uncouple left screw until |A−B| ≤ 0.003″. Lock home dog. Record the reading.
  12. A12 Test panel + pass schedule — 80 / 120 / 180. Caliper four corners. If scatter > 0.003″, re-clock. Finish at 0.001″. Optional oscillator last.
