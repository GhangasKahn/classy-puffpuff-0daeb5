# 3D-print joint coupons (1:1, PLA or PETG)

Print these **before** milling 4×6 / oak. Units in the STL are **millimetres**.

| File | Joint | Critical fit |
|---|---|---|
| `T-501_kusabi.stl` | W-001 kusabi blank ⅝″ × 1⅛″ × 5½″ | Taper on the bench after print; confirm reverse-drive in a scrap mortise |
| `T-502_tenon.stl` | Post foot tenon 2.50 × 4.50 × 3.50 | Must drop into F-003 and lift out |
| `W-003_pivot.stl` | Oak pivot ⌀1.25″ × 4″ | Locational in sill + soffit; lift-off +Z |

Do not treat these meshes as structural. They are fit gauges.
