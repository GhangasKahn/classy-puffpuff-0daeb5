02 — Three ideas, one fence

Prairie face. Temple joints. Removable feet.

Prairie · Darwin Martin

Tree of Life light-screen

Cantilevered 2×12 eave, belts that stick past Roman-brick piers, and three recessed lights: nested squares / three stylized trees / nested squares. Solid water table. 1.50″ max aperture. Wright at a distance; craft up close.

Japan · hand joinery

Nuki + kusabi

Rails pass through the posts and lock with hardwood wedges. Gate uses drawbored mortise & tenon. Cap scarfs with kama-tsugi over the mid post. No nails, no screws, no plates in the timber.

Buffalo · winter logic

Lift and store

Posts drop into F-003 cross-ties on a timber ladder that sits on the driveway. Two live planters ballast the privacy bays — none at the house. Knock wedges, withdraw bands, lift posts, empty troughs. Store dry until spring. No holes, no cement, no pad.

06 — Build sequence

Cut dry. Fit. Paint. Set. Drop in.

Phase 1

Site — no digging

Measure the 143″ opening on the driveway, house siding to existing fence. Place rubber pads. Do not excavate. Do not pour. Gate goes against the house.

Phase 2

Timber ladder

Half-lap two 4×6 dodai sills to four cross-ties. Peg. Sit on the slab. Build two garden-side planters for the privacy bays — none at the gate. Posts tenon 3.50″ into the ties.

Phase 3

Light-screen

Through-nuki water table + two belts. Mill Tree of Life and nested-rect cassettes. Roman-brick pier wrap. 2×12 eave with kama-tsugi over P2, fascia, soffit dado for IP67 tape.

Phase 4

Gate & latch

Tree of Life portal — matching lights, not a Z-brace on the garden face. Oak pivots at P1 (weight into the sill). Sliding oak latch into P0. Confirm swing. Do not epoxy into the house.

Phase 5

Paint & plant

Ease 1/16″, end-grain sealer, PT dry then prime, two owner-gray coats, extra on planter interiors. Mask locking faces. Drop posts, re-insert bands, hang gate, plant troughs, light the cap.

  • Winter reverse: wedges → bands → gate off pivots → posts → empty troughs → store dry
  • Spring: reverse again; re-seat wedges snug, not brutal; replant
