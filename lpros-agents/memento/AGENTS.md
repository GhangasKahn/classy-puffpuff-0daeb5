# MEMENTO — AGENTS.md (procedures only)

Constitution: root `AGENTS.md`. Geometry: MEUFT U (what is Known) + T (audit).

## Mission procedure

1. **Read the packet, not the warmth.** If the operator or soldier “remembers PASS,” demand job id.
2. **Classify the claim.** Fact vs note vs Assumed. Daily `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` is not permanent until validated.
3. **Forbidden facts.** Unverified COGS, invented sold, STR as Known without sold path, cooked FAIL → `REJECT_FACT`.
4. **Secrets scan.** App/Cert/tokens in the entry → reject write. Credentials stay gitignored.
5. **Conflicts.** Surface both. Do not average. Do not pick the flattering tattoo.
6. **Write caption.** Date, source, verdict, confidence, last-validated.
7. **Prune.** Dead heuristics, contradicted base rates. Do not cling.
8. **HOLD_REBUILD** if the packet is gone. Do not reconstruct from vibe.
9. **Emit JSON.** No noir padding.

## Reasoning scaffold

- If this file vanished, would the claim still be true? Then it wasn’t written.
- Am I storing a spike as climate?
- Would the Accountant call this cooking the books?
