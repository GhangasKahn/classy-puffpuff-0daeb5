01 / THE REFUSAL

The governing decision

The gram is not the enemy. The compromise is.

A fine fish edge wants a narrow, low-friction profile. A marking knife wants a dead-flat reference face. Controlled trail carving asks for more support behind the apex. Calling one blade “universal” does not resolve those opposing demands.

KAGE proposes a shared titanium handle and lock, then gives each cut its own inspectable geometry. Carry only the module the day requires.

05 / EVIDENCE BEFORE LEGEND

What is known / what is not

A proposal earns confidence by exposing its unknowns.

This study uses real manufacturing and material precedents where available. It does not convert those precedents into claims about an unbuilt knife.

Verified precedent External source

Titanium-laminated cutting tools exist in Zen-Wu’s current vocabulary.

Zen-Wu’s official plane-blade listings describe cutting steels laminated into titanium alloy bodies. That supports a manufacturing conversation; it does not prove this lock, mass, edge rail, or sanitation design.

Material evidence External source

MagnaMax is a candidate, not a conclusion.

Niagara Specialty Metals describes MagnaMax as a high-wear powder-metallurgy stainless steel and publishes heat-treatment guidance. Thin-edge stability, batch hardness, corrosion after finishing, and maker access still require direct testing.

Read the manufacturer data sheet ↗

Market benchmark External source

The lightweight reference is measurable.

Cold Steel lists the 3.5-inch Bird & Game at 1.4 oz. KAGE’s proposed loadouts are compared only as modeled mass—not as proven superiority in cutting, ergonomics, or durability.

View the official benchmark ↗

Component precedent External source

Wire diameter must follow the fish.

Belmont’s official 80 cm shinkeijime line is offered in 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, and 1.5 mm diameters. That supports a modular wire study; suitability for a species and technique remains the user’s responsibility.

06 / MAKE THE UNKNOWN MEASURABLE

Prototype gates

The next object is not inventory.
It is evidence.

  1. 01

    Geometry lock

    Resolve tang capture, hand clearance, edge zones, drainage, and left/right-handed module architecture in CAD.

    OUTPUT: CONTROLLED DRAWINGS
  2. 02

    Four engineering samples

    Two dominant-hand and two mirrored samples. Record actual mass, flatness, hardness, lock wear, and sheath retention.

    OUTPUT: MEASURED BASELINE
  3. 03

    Abuse the assumptions

    Wet-glove control, fish residue cleanup, salt exposure, edge stability, wood registration, drop, flex, and accidental light bone contact.

    OUTPUT: FAILURE LOG
  4. 04

    Quote only what survived

    Revise before discussing 25, 50, or 100-unit production. No preorder, scarcity, or launch claim precedes sample acceptance.

    OUTPUT: PILOT DECISION
