Proposed instrument · not for sale here

A knife that can account for itself.

SHINOBI//82 KAGE is a modular shop-and-river edge for people who weigh a pack in grams and a joint in thousandths. One titanium kusabi frame. Three honest blades. Nothing seated that cannot name its job.

Act II · Recognition

The gram is a moral unit.

A Hyperlite pack does not forgive a second knife you never draw. A marking knife that lives in the shop does not forgive being asked to open a trout. A bird-and-trout knife that lives in polymer does not forgive a kumiko shoulder.

Most “do-everything” blades solve this by lying. They thicken the spine until fish skin smears. They shorten the belly until elk seam work becomes sawing. They wrap the handle in something that holds blood. They call the result ultralight because the box is small.

  • The shop wants a reference face. A dead-flat back that registers to a square. Zen-Wu already builds this language into a Shirabiki-style marking knife and into urade-fuyo plane blades that refuse a hollow back.
  • The river wants release and rinse. Skin that parts without tearing. A frame with no trapped cavities. A sheath that drains. A sharpening surface that does not stay in the fish box.
  • The pack wants a number you can defend. Not “featherweight.” A line-item mass. If a module cannot justify its grams, it stays in the studio chest.

Act III · Revelation

Seat the kusabi. Read the ledger.

The signature of this system is not a silhouette. It is a wedge lock: one open titanium frame, one blade at a time, accessories that clip to the saya instead of hiding in foam. The numbers below are a mass model. They are not weighed prototypes.

What this does: choose a hand, seat one blade, add only the modules you will carry. The gram total and the task list update together so capability cannot drift away from mass. Example: River day seats MIZU-82 with the diamond tag, tweezer, and S-50 wire.

What this loadout will and will not do

Three geometries · one frame

Do not ask one grind to tell three truths.

The frame is shared. The edge is not. Each module is written for a job that the others would falsify if they pretended to replace it.

MIZU-82

River, food, and close game

Eighty-two millimetres of low-belly edge for trout, panfish, catfish, salmon breakdown, and seam work on deer or elk. Proposed steel: MagnaMax, a powder-metallurgy stainless published by Larrin Thomas as a higher-wear companion to MagnaCut, not a Zen-Wu production steel today. Proposed grind: 60/40 hamaguri, mirrored for the opposite hand. The tip is for tracing, not for punching a skull.

Blade 82 mm
Apex target 12° / 14°
Heel reinforced 15° / 17°
Behind-edge 0.10–0.13 mm
Food-contact: rinse-through frame
KUMIKO-42

A marking land that stays a land

Forty-two millimetres, perfectly flat back, 15° single flat bevel. This is the module that inherits Zen-Wu’s published travel-knife idea: flat backs and flat bevels for scribing, chamfering, and fitting kumiko. Proposed edge: ZW-V1 or MagnaCut, only if the workshop chooses a steel it already heat-treats. Right and left are separate tools.

NATA-60

Controlled chopping. Not batoning.

Sixty millimetres, 24° single bevel, MagnaCut candidate. Whittling, notching, feathering, and chisel-style cuts in camp wood. It is not an axe, not a splitter, and not a pry bar. If the work needs a saw or a hatchet, the honest move is to carry one.

Material atelier

Exotic is allowed only when it outlasts fashion.

The visual world is JDM tackle discipline — Megabass-level obsession with load paths and finish — translated into a toolworks object. No copied silhouettes. No plastic. No material seated because a catalog called it exotic.

Graphene was researched and declined as a specified coating. Published titanium–graphene composites exist in laboratories. They are not a drop-in film we can promise on a food-contact knife. DLC remains the proposed body finish because it is an established vapor-deposited carbon film, not a slogan.

Ti-6Al-4V Keep Frame, wedge, collet, tweezer, tag substrate. Zen-Wu already uses titanium-alloy bodies on the marking knife, PalmBlade, planes, and block plane.
CPM MagnaCut Precedent Published on Zen-Wu’s Shirabiki-style marking knife and titanium PalmBlade. Hardness listed by Dictum at 62–63 HRC for that marking knife. Candidate for NATA-60.
MagnaMax Propose Developer testing places wear resistance above MagnaCut with similar corrosion behavior in a 1% saltwater test. Wide commercial release was described as Q2 2026. Not a Zen-Wu catalog steel. Availability and heat treatment must be confirmed.
ZW-V1 / ZW-C1 Propose Zen-Wu’s own edges: ZW-V1 laminated into titanium on Japanese-style planes and travel knives; ZW-C1 carbon on the C-1. Honest candidates for KUMIKO-42 if the workshop prefers a steel it already owns.
Dyneema SK99 Propose UHMWPE tether. Floats. High tenacity. Melts around 140°C and can be cut by sharp hardware, so the aperture must be radiused and the fiber kept off the edge. Not a handle wrap. Not in the food-contact stack.
Kiri, honoki, kurogaki, urushi Propose Saya and optional contact inlays. Wash-through. No Kydex, G10, FRN, silicone, or resin-stabilized wood in the carried system.
Nickel diamond Propose 600 / 1200 faces on the tag, plus a replaceable 1 μm film. Zen-Wu already sells diamond plates and publishes a grit-conversion table. The tag is also the ikejime T-handle — the knife point is not.
Nitinol wires Propose S-50 for trout and most bass; L-80 for adult Niagara salmon. A single compromised diameter is refused. Shape-memory shinkeijime wires in these sizes already exist from Japanese makers such as Belmont.
Graphene film Declined Not specified. Insufficient production evidence for a food-contact or edge-retention claim. Revisit only if a named coating process can be tested beside DLC.

Act IV · Proof

What is already true, and what is still a drawing.

Zen-Wu, as published

Zen-Wu (参物) is a Wuhan toolworks of about seventeen people, with fewer than ten EDM machines and fewer than ten large CNCs, close to aerospace steels and robotic heat treatment. Luke L. is listed as Master of eye — product development, research, philosophy, visual, branding, channels, IT, robotics and AI. zenwutoolworks.com/pages/about · retrieved 15 Aug 2026

The Shirabiki-style marking knife is a titanium-alloy handle with a dovetail-laminated Magnacut edge and a flame-pattern hollow on the back. Zen-Wu describes it as about the weight of a pencil. A European retailer lists 165 mm overall, 40 g, Magnacut, 62–63 HRC. zenwutoolworks.com/products/shirabiki · Dictum 745313 · retrieved 15 Aug 2026

Travel knives are sold with flat backs and flat bevels for whittling, chamfering, kumiko, shop and trail use. C-1 uses ZW-C1 carbon. Q-1, L-1, and J-1 use dovetail-laminated ZW-V1. Japanese-style plane blades are urade-fuyo: no hollow, because the factory back is already flat. Travel knives · Japanese-style planes · retrieved 15 Aug 2026

Steel, mass, and the mass we will not claim

MagnaMax is described by its developer as keeping MagnaCut’s corrosion and hardness character while raising wear resistance, with no rust spots in a 1% saltwater test and a toughness–retention balance compared to K390 / Vanadis 8. That is laboratory language from Knife Steel Nerds, not a SHINOBI field result. Knife Steel Nerds, 19 Jan 2026

Cold Steel publishes the Bird & Game at 3.5 in / 1.4 oz, AUS-8A, polymer grip, Secure-Ex sheath. It is the honest mass benchmark for a small field knife — and the construction SHINOBI refuses: polymer, a single grind, no shop reference face. coldsteel.com/bird-game · retrieved 15 Aug 2026

There are no customer testimonials here. No production hardness tickets. No weighed samples. No accepted purchase order. If those arrive, they replace this paragraph. Until then the strongest proof is precedent plus an inspectable model.

Refused work

No batoning. No prying. No pelvis splitting. No chopping antler or heavy bone. No screwdriver use of the point. No driving the knife through a fish skull. No long one-pass salmon fillet as a promised behavior.

Ikejime, named correctly

The dedicated spike lives in the sharpening tag and locks across the saya as a T-handle. Species have different brain locations; confirm with a current ikijime reference before any use. Large salmon or thick-skulled catfish may need a full-size spike. This page is not a slaughter manual.

What happens after a brief

A conversation. Then, if anyone agrees to make it: engineering samples, wet and shop tests, and only then a pilot quantity. No deposit is taken on this page. No scarcity clock is running.
