    Physics

    What the plate actually computes

    Look angles from observer and ISS geodetic positions, WGS-84 ellipsoid. East / north / up from the ECEF difference vector. Azimuth is atan2(E, N), elevation atan2(U, √(E²+N²)), range the vector magnitude.

    Passes from Celestrak GP TLE + satellite.js SGP4, sampled every 30 s for 36 h. AOS / LOS at 10° elevation. Naked-eye requires elevation ≥ 10°, observer sun altitude < −6°, and the station outside Earth’s cylindrical umbra.

    Magnitude is an estimate: −1.8 + 5 log₁₀(range / 1000 km) plus a phase term from the sun–station–observer angle. Labeled EST. Not a photometric measurement.

    Fist rule on FACE: one fist at arm’s length ≈ 10°. Field orientation, not a survey.
