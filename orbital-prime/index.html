    Astrodynamics & Coordinate Transforms

    Look angles are computed in real time from geodetic observer and sub-satellite coordinates on the WGS-84 reference ellipsoid. Topocentric East, North, Up (ENU) frame vectors are extracted from Earth-Centered Earth-Fixed (ECEF) deltas. Azimuth is derived via atan2(E, N), elevation via atan2(U, √(E²+N²)), and Euclidean range from vector norm.

    Pass predictions execute client-side through satellite.js SGP4 propagation against Celestrak General Perturbations TLE sets, evaluated at 30-second steps over 36 hours. Naked-eye passes require topocentric elevation ≥ 10°, solar depression angle < −6° (nautical twilight or darker), and an illuminated satellite state outside Earth's umbral shadow cylinder.

    Photometric visual magnitude estimation follows: −1.8 + 5 log₁₀(Range / 1000 km) + Φ(phase) accounting for station-sun-observer scattering angles.
